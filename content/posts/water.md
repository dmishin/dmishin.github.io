Hydrogen burns to water: \ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}. Lime is \ce{Ca(OH)2}.

<!--more-->

Inside math, MathJax's mhchem does the job: $\ce{SO4^2-}$.

The energy is $E = mc^2$, and in display form:

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$
